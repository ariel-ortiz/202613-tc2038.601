### Key Transfer Hubs

* **Cuauhtémoc**: Connects **Line 1** and **Line 2**.
* **Félix U. Gómez**: Connects **Line 1** and **Line 3**.
* **General I. Zaragoza**: Connects **Line 2** and **Line 3**.

*(Note: Lines 2 and 3 operate continuously as a single service route, allowing passengers traveling between Sendero and Hospital Metropolitano to do so without physically changing trains at Zaragoza).*
